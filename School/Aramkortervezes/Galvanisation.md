How to Galvanize DIY PCB

**DIY PCB Galvanization (Through-Hole Plating)**

To galvanize a DIY PCB—specifically to deposit copper inside via holes—follow this proven method using electroless plating and electroplating:

### 1. **Prepare the PCB**
- **Drill holes** using a precision drill (e.g., tungsten carbide bit) to ensure clean, burr-free holes.
- **Clean thoroughly**: Sand the copper surface with fine abrasive paper (e.g., 600+ grit), then wash with dish soap and rinse. Inspect holes under light to ensure no debris remains.

### 2. **Activate the Holes (Critical Step)**
- Use an **activator solution** to make the non-conductive inner walls of the holes conductive:
  - Mix **copper sulfate**, **calcium hypophosphite**, **ammonium hydroxide**, and **liquid soap** in distilled water.
  - This solution deposits nano-sized copper particles on the hole walls via thermal decomposition.
  - **Soak the PCB** in the activator for 2–3 seconds, then dry it.

### 3. **Electroplate Copper**
- **Set up an electroplating bath**:
  - Use a **plastic container** with a **zinc anode** (or copper if plating copper).
  - Use a **DC power supply** (3–6V, adjustable current).
  - Use a **zinc sulfate or copper sulfate solution** as the electrolyte.
- **Connect the PCB as the cathode** (negative terminal) and the anode (positive) to the power supply.
- **Immerse the PCB** in the bath, ensuring the anode and cathode don’t touch.
- **Apply current** (e.g., 0.5–1 A/dm²) for 15–30 minutes to deposit copper inside the via holes and on the surface.

### 4. **Post-Processing**
- Rinse the PCB thoroughly with water.
- Dry and inspect. For better results, use **pulse plating** (reverse current cycles) to improve copper uniformity.

> ⚠️ **Note**: This method avoids expensive palladium-based processes and works well for home PCB makers. However, **calcium hypophosphite is restricted in the U.S.** due to DEA regulations—check local laws before sourcing.

### Alternative: Electroless Copper Plating (Simpler, No Power Supply)
- Use a **commercial electroless copper kit** (e.g., from eBay or electronics suppliers).
- Follow the kit instructions: clean, activate, then dip in the plating solution. The copper deposits spontaneously on the activated surface.

> ✅ **Pro Tip**: For best results, design your PCB with **balanced copper distribution** to avoid uneven plating (a key concern in industrial processes).
